03 — From our hands to yours

How we make
the magic.

Good things take time. We pour a little love into every step so your BloomBite arrives as special as the person receiving it.

01
♧

STEP 1: PREPARATION

Gather all ingredients and tools. Measure everything accurately. This ensures consistent taste and no waste.

02
◌

STEP 2: MIXING THE BATTER

Combine dry ingredients first flour, cocoa, sugar, baking powder. Then add wet ingredients eggs, oil, water, and vanilla. Mix gently until smooth.

03
✿

STEP 3: BAKING

Pour batter into greased mini pans. Bake at the correct temperature until a toothpick comes out clean. Let cool completely before decorating.

04
♡

STEP 4: DECORATING & WRAPPING

Frost and decorate the cooled cake. Wrap carefully in the bouquet-style packaging and tie with a ribbon. Add a small tag or sticker.

05
♥

STEP 5: HANDOVER TO CUSTOMER AND ENJOY!
